Grooved Bottom Options
Slide-in bottom: grooved into Side 1, Side 2 & End 1 (with Dovetail joints, End 1's groove has to be cut from its other side - tick "Reverse End 1 (Front) for groove on own sheet" to get it on a sheet of its own, otherwise it is skipped). End 2 is shortened to the top of the groove, so the panel can always slide in/out there, even after glue-up. Uncheck "Bottom" under Faces To Machine below to keep the groove but skip cutting the panel itself (e.g. to slide in your own material).
